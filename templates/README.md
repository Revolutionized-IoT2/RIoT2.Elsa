# Workflow templates

Workflows to import on RIoT2.UI's *Workflows* page (**Import**). Each imports as a draft
with placeholder selections: choose your own reports and commands in its RIoT activities, then
publish. How to use them and the activities: the [automation guide](https://github.com/Revolutionized-IoT2/.github/blob/main/docs/guides/automation.md#templates).

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `motion-light.json` | Motion turns a light on; it goes off five minutes after the last motion |
| `price-based-heater.json` | A heater goes off above an electricity price of 15 and on again below 10 |
| `forecast-preheat.json` | When tomorrow's prices arrive and tomorrow is cold (below 3 °C), today is cheaper and it is at most 20 °C inside, the heat pump goes to 22 °C in today's remaining cheapest price slots (2.1.0) |
| `llm-heat-schedule.json` | When the next day's temperature forecast drops below 3 °C, a language model plans the heat pump's temperatures (18–22 °C) from prices, the forecast and the inside temperature (2.1.0) |
| `sauna-ready-announcement.json` | When the sauna gets above 80 °C, a Google Cast speaker says "Sauna is ready"; it speaks again after the sauna has cooled below 60 °C (needs the Google Cast speaker device, RIoT2.Net.Devices 3.0.0) |

The files are generated by `RIoT2.Elsa.Tests/WorkflowTemplates.cs`; don't edit them by hand. Run the
tests with `RIOT2_UPDATE_TEMPLATES=1` to write them again.
